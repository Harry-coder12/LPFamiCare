Features

Everything the family needs,
on one calm screen

Simple enough for a busy day, thorough enough for a GP appointment.

01 / 04
Today

See every dose at a glance.

The Today screen shows what's due and what's been taken, so everyone knows where things stand without a phone call.

  • Doses marked Taken, skipped or refused
  • Weekly adherence at a glance
  • Quick Log it button for daily notes
02 / 04
Medicines

Never miss a dose or refill.

Add a medicine once. FamiCare builds the schedule, reminds you when each dose is due and counts how many tablets are left.

  • Reminders on the household clock
  • Tablet counter for refills
  • One-tap GP report
03 / 04
Care log

Log how the day went.

Choose what kind of entry: note, mood, vitals, eating, sleep, a fall, behaviour, medicine, an appointment, a handover or an incident.

  • Vitals such as blood pressure and weight
  • Add a photo to an entry
  • Handover notes for other carers
04 / 04
Together

One household. Everyone on the same page.

Share a household with family, keep the people's details, emergency card and contacts in one place, and coordinate tasks.

  • Who we care for: names and allergies
  • Members and contacts, like GP and pharmacy
  • Assign and track care tasks

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? We usually reply within a day.

Contact us
Who is FamiCare for?

Families, partners and friends looking after an ageing parent or loved one who takes regular medicines.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything is saved on your phone first, so it keeps working with no signal.

Can several family members use it?

Yes. Create a household after signing in and invite others to share the same care record.

Does FamiCare give medical advice?

No. It helps you remember and record. Always check with a GP, pharmacist or carer about medicines.

Is it free?

You can download it free from Google Play. Some features may be part of a plan, shown clearly in the app.
